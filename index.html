Loading show data…

All show schedules, service colors and prices live in shows.json. On start the page uses, in order: a file you imported (kept in this browser), then the shows.json next to this page, then its built-in copy. Update shows always loads the online shows.json.

To pull fresh schedules yourself, run python update_data.py next to your shows.json and tracklist.json. It refreshes your tracked titles and adds new seasons from TVmaze. With a free TMDB key it also adds new movies. Then use Import JSON to load the result.

      Rates & discounts

      Loading rates…

      Each service has a standard monthly rate and optional discounts. Two kinds of discount: intro (a promo price for your first few monthly charges if you subscribe before a deadline) and until a date (a promo price on every charge up to an end date, then the standard rate again). Only discounts with “Use this discount” checked change your plan. Your edits save in this browser. If none are saved, the page loads the default rates.json next to it, or else its built-in copy.

        About the data

        Streaming services change dates often. Dates marked estimated are educated guesses based on announced windows and each show’s usual pattern. Billing assumes monthly plans that renew on the same day each month, and that canceling keeps your access until the paid month ends (true for all eight services at the time of writing). Free trials and bundles aren’t included.
